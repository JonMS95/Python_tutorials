In the quiet town of Willowbrook, the streets smelled faintly of fresh-baked bread every morning, thanks to Mrs. Maple’s tiny bakery on the corner. People often said her croissants had a magical way of making you forget your worries, if only for a moment.
One particularly breezy Tuesday, a curious squirrel named Chester began visiting the bakery, perching on the windowsill and watching the townspeople with what seemed like polite interest. 
Soon enough, Chester became something of a local celebrity, known for his impeccable timing—always arriving just as the first tray of warm pastries came out. Children would wave at him, adults would chuckle, and even the postman would pause to toss him a crumb or two.
Somehow, in that small corner of the world, a bakery and a squirrel reminded everyone that magic wasn’t just in fairy tales—it could be found in tiny, everyday moments too.
